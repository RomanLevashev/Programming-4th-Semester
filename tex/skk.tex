\textbf{Задача.} Доказать, что
\[
S\,K\,K = I.
\]

\[
S \equiv \lambda x\,y\,z.\, x\,z\,(y\,z),
\qquad
K \equiv \lambda x\,y.\,x,
\qquad
I \equiv \lambda x.\,x.
\]

\[
S\,K\,K
=
((\lambda x\,y\,z.\, x\,z\,(y\,z))\,(\lambda x\,y.\,x))\,(\lambda x\,y.\,x)
\]

\[
\to_\beta
(\lambda y\,z.\, (\lambda x\,y.\,x)\,z\,(y\,z))\,(\lambda x\,y.\,x)
\]

\[
\to_\beta
\lambda z.\, (\lambda x\,y.\,x)\,z\,((\lambda x\,y.\,x)\,z)
\]

\[
\to_\beta
\lambda z.\, (\lambda y.\,z)\,((\lambda x\,y.\,x)\,z)
\]

\[
\to_\beta
\lambda z.\, z
\]

\[
= I
\]
